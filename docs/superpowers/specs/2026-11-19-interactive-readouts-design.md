# The interactive readouts of a model app

## Intent

A physiologically based model has hundreds of readouts and an app declares a handful of them. The glimepiride app shows four: the parent and its two metabolites in the arm vein and the two metabolites in the urine. Everything else the model computes, the concentration in the liver, the flux of the metabolism by CYP2C9, the amount in the feces, is in the model and out of reach of the reader.

The figure of the model already is the index of all of it. It is generated by pkpdsim from the definition and every meaningful group of it carries a `data-k` key: the organs (`li`, `ki`, `gu`), the substances (`sub:gli`, `sub:gli_m1`), the routes, the sample, the lumen, the urine, the feces and a badge per process. The figure is therefore the control the dashboard is missing: a reader clicks a place in the body, the timecourses of what the model reports there appear beside it, a second click takes them away, and the picture marks what is on screen.

This design makes that interaction a capability of an app, so the single file page the factory of pkdb_models writes carries it as the documentation page does, and so the next model with a figure declares one instead of writing one.

## Goals

A click on an element of the model figure adds the panes of the readouts of that element, a click on a marked element removes them, and every key which is on screen is marked in the figure.

Everything the figure shows is reachable: every organ with every substance in the form that organ holds it in, the sample, the lumen, the urine, the feces, the routes and the processes.

One card holds what applies to every plot: the logarithmic axis, the reset, the draws of the uncertainty, and the information of the last run, which is the seconds it took and the engine which ran it.

The parameter table of the dashboard shows the coefficient of variation of every parameter next to its standard deviation.

The dashboard is one page which ends with the citation of the model, and the prose which is not part of the interaction moves into a tab of the app.

The whole thing is declared, not scripted: an app declares its figure and the panels its keys select, and the renderer knows nothing about glimepiride.

## Non-goals

No new simulation capability. A simulate request names the values of the knobs, the grid, the variant and the draws, and nothing else; the curves of a panel are what its declaration says. A readout a page can plot is a declared panel, and this design declares more panels rather than changing the contract.

The parameter table stays the dashboard's own JavaScript. It is the pkpdutils kernel in the browser and it is not moving into the renderer, so the factory page keeps having no table.

No control over the simulated time span. The settings card reports how long the last run took; it does not change how long the model runs, which no request can express today.

No prose in the declaration. The information tab is built from what an app already carries, which is its title, its subtitle, its citation, its DOI, its licenses, its variants and the versions of what simulated it.

## Anti-goals

A dashboard which simulates once per pane. The panes of a page share one scan of the model, and a selection of twenty readouts has to stay one run. A design which multiplies runs with panes has failed even if every pane is correct.

A dead hotspot. A key which is clickable and reports nothing, or a pane without a unit or with an axis nobody can read, is worse than a picture which is not clickable at all.

The renderer knowing the naming convention of pkpdsim. `li` plus `sub:gli` becoming `LI__Cpl_gli` is knowledge of the model generator, and a renderer which guesses it will be wrong for the next model.

A page a browser struggles with. The factory page is one file which already carries a compiled model and a sample of draws, and the figure and sixty declarations must not be what makes it too heavy to open.

## Constraints

The panel contract is fixed for this work. `SimulateRequest` carries `values`, `grid`, `variant` and `draws`, the bundle endpoint rejects any other field, a panel declares at most 16 curves and 8 knobs, and no limit bounds the number of pages or panels of an app.

The compiled model exposes 1228 selections with their units, among them every per organ concentration, the unbound ones, the arm vein observables, the excretion amounts and rates, and the fluxes of the processes. Everything this design plots exists in the model already.

The figure keys are pkpdsim codes and not SBML ids, and the tissue form of a substance in an organ (`none`, `merged`, `full`, `organ`, see `pkpdsim.pbpk.parts.Parts` and `OrganContent.parts`) is what decides which compartments of that organ report it.

The factory page is one self contained HTML file, so the figure cannot be a file next to the page: it travels inline in the payload of the app.

Our libraries travel at their latest release, so the order is pkpdsim, then sbmlsim-web, then sbmlsim which vendors the renderer and carries the declaration, then pkdb_models.

The page computes no statistic. The kernel answers `cv` among its summary statistics, so the new column is a column of the kernel's own number.

## Approaches

**Where the map from a figure key to the readouts of the model is written.** Three places were considered.

In pkpdsim, beside the hover cards. `visual/interactive.py` already builds a card per key from a `ModelContent` which knows every organ, the substances it holds and in which form, the routes, the sample, the lumen, the urine and the feces. A `readouts(content)` next to `cards(content, volumes)` is the same walk over the same structure, and the figure writer emits it as a sidecar of the SVG. Every model with a figure gets a clickable one and the convention stays where it is invented. It costs a release of pkpdsim before the rest of the chain can move.

In pkdb_models, for glimepiride alone. The model package derives the map from its own definition. One release fewer, and the next model writes the same code again with the organ and substance codes of pkpdsim copied into it.

In the renderer, by convention. sbmlsim-web reads the keys and matches them against the selections of the model. Two repositories, and the layer which must stay model agnostic becomes the one which knows the convention.

The first was chosen. The cost is one release in a chain which is released often anyway, and the alternative puts a generator's private convention into either a model package or a renderer.

**How a clicked readout becomes a pane.** Two ways were considered.

A declared panel per readout group, shown or hidden by the selection. The declaration carries the label, the unit, the axis and the scale of every curve, which is what makes a pane readable, and the page shows the subset the reader has selected. The run is shared, so sixty declared panels cost declaration size and one scan.

A field of the request which names the curves. A page could then plot any selection of the model without a declaration. It needs a new field in the contract, validation of arbitrary ids, a release of sbmlsim, sbmlsim-core and the renderer, and it still leaves the renderer inventing a label, a unit and an axis for an id it has never seen.

The first was chosen, which the contract also forces today.

## What is decided

**pkpdsim writes the map.** `readouts(content)` in `pbpk/visual/interactive.py`, beside the `cards(content, volumes)` which already walks the same structure, returns the readouts of every key of the figure: the selection id, a label, the unit and the kind of the quantity (a concentration, an amount, a rate or a flux). It composes what the package already owns, which is the id convention of `pbpk/ids.py` (`Cpl_<s>`, `Cu_<part>_<s>`, the organ prefix of the flat model) and the vocabulary of `pbpk/observers.py` (`readout_ids`, the urinary rate of the kidney, the lumen amounts and the absorption fluxes of the intestine). An organ key yields one group per substance it holds, with the compartments of its form: `none` gives the plasma, `merged` adds the tissue and its unbound concentration, `full` adds the interstitium, the cells and their unbound concentrations. The sample yields the total, the free and the whole blood concentration of every substance, the urine the amounts and the rates, the lumen and the feces their amounts, and a badge of a process the flux of its reaction. The figure writer of `pbpk/figure.py` writes it beside the SVG as a JSON sidecar which names its contract, `figurereadouts/1`, as every payload of this family names its own.

The sidecar lists every key the figure draws, including the ones which are decoration of the drawing (`bladder`, `gallbladder`, `uterus`), whose readouts are an empty list. A key of the figure which the sidecar does not mention at all is a defect of pkpdsim and fails its own test, which is what keeps a hotspot from going dead.

**A key is a path, not a word.** A substance chip sits inside the group of its organ, so the readouts of glimepiride in the liver are the pair of the two keys and not either alone. The sidecar is keyed by the path of the keys from the outside in, `li/sub:gli`, and the renderer builds that path by walking the `data-k` values of the ancestors of what was clicked and falling back to the shorter path when the longer one is not in the map. The renderer therefore joins strings and knows no convention.

**sbmlsim turns the sidecar into a declaration.** pkpdsim does not depend on sbmlsim, and sbmlsim owns the declaration types, so the step belongs there and the sidecar is the data contract between the two, as the bundle is the contract between sbmlsim and sbmlsim-core. `panels_from_figure` reads an SVG and its sidecar and returns the panels of every group together with the figure of the app: the SVG inline, its title, the credit of the drawing, and the map from a key path to the ids of the panels it selects. `App` carries that figure, `AppPage` says whether it shows it and which of its panels are on screen before anything is clicked, and the app payload, the export and the single file page carry it as declared. Every selection id is checked against the compiled model when the bundle is written, and an id the model does not have fails the build.

One panel per place and substance is at most five curves, which is the `full` form, so the limit of sixteen curves is not in the way and no group has to be split. The inline SVG is bounded at 512 KB, which the anatomical drawing of glimepiride (200 KB) fits inside and which keeps the single file page from growing without a bound.

**sbmlsim-web renders and selects.** A `sbmlsim-figure` element draws the SVG of a declaration, marks the key paths which are on screen and reports the path of a click. The app composes it beside its grid, holds the selection in an attribute and reports a change of it in an event, and renders the panels of the selection in the order the page declares them, whatever order they were clicked in. The shared card holds the logarithmic axis of every plot, the reset, the draws and the information of the last run, which is the seconds it took and the engine which ran it, both read from the provenance a run already carries. The logarithmic setting sets the axis of every panel whose curves share one scale and leaves a panel of mixed scales to its own, which is what the panel already enforces. The reset keeps the selection and resets the knobs, as it does today, and the per panel reset and per panel logarithmic switch stay where they are. The information of an app becomes a tab built from the declaration and from that provenance, so nothing new has to be authored to fill it.

The panes of the selection are the panels which are rendered, and a panel which is not rendered runs nothing: that is what keeps a selection of twenty readouts at one scan of the model, since the panes of one state share their run. A test counts the requests of a selection and holds that claim, because it is the anti-goal of this design.

**pkdb_models declares it.** Glimepiride reads the sidecar of its anatomical drawing at import, as its panels are already built at import, and declares the panels of every group, the figure with the key map and the four panels of today as the selection a reader starts from. Nothing is generated into the repository. The anatomical drawing is the one it declares, since it carries the keys of the sample and of the routes next to the organs and the substances. Its citation, its DOI and its licenses are declared already and the footer shows them.

**The documentation dashboard.** The parameter table gains the coefficient of variation of the kernel next to the standard deviation, which is `cv` added to the statistics the page asks `summarize` for, the control of the draws moves into the shared card, and the citation of the app is the last thing on the page.

## What is left to the builder

How many panes sit in a row at the small size, and how the figure and the panes share the width of a phone.

The wording of the label of a generated panel, which comes from the name of the substance and the compartment it is reported in.

How a marked key reads in both themes, which has to work on the schematic and on the anatomical drawing.

Whether the selection of a reader survives a reload of the documentation page, which this design does not ask for and does not forbid.
