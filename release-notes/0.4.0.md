# Release notes for sbmlsim-web 0.4.0

A curve carries the band of the uncertainty of its model, a page may ask for the draws of it, and the glimepiride example is the dashboard of what that buys: four small panels with their bands and a table of the pharmacokinetic parameters of the very draws the panels shade.

## Features

- **The band of a curve** ([#6](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim-web/pull/6)). A panel whose declaration carries an uncertainty (`PanelUncertainty`: the draws a request without a number gets, the draws of the sample of the endpoint, the level of the band and the seed it was drawn with) asks its endpoint for that many draws and shades the percentiles the answer carries under every curve, in the colour of the curve. The band travels for every point of the grid, so it follows a knob as the curve does and not behind it, and `drawn().bands` hands the shaded range to a readout of the page.
- **The attribute `draws`** ([#6](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim-web/pull/6)). `<sbmlsim-panel draws="32">` and `<sbmlsim-app draws="32">` are how many draws a page asks for: absent is the number the declaration states, `0` is no band, a number above the sample of the endpoint is that maximum, and a panel of a model which declares no uncertainty draws no band whatever it says. A change of it runs the panel again, since the draws are part of what a run answers; one control of a page moves the bands of its figures together.
- **The parameters of the curves of an example, computed in the page** ([#5](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim-web/pull/5)). The glimepiride example reads the figure a panel draws (`drawn()`, on `sbmlsim-shown`) and sends it to the kernel of pkpdutils, `pkpdutils-core` on npm, which is the non-compartmental analysis of that library as a WebAssembly module: C~max~, t~max~, AUC, the terminal half-life, MRT, CL/F and V~z~/F of every analyte, next to the simulation which produced the curve and without a server.

## Changes

- **The example is a dashboard** ([#6](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim-web/pull/6)). The four panels are small and side by side, their subtitles name the site of the sample alone, and the table under them states the spread of the draws next to every parameter: the value of the model, the standard deviation of the draws and a box of their spread against their own median (the bar their middle half, the whisker the draws within one and a half of it, one axis for the whole table). A band of a figure is no distribution of a parameter, so the page asks the endpoint for the curves of the draws themselves, which travel for a request of a single point, analyses all of them in one batch of the kernel and reduces that table with `summarize` of the same kernel; no statistic is computed in the page. The control `draws of the fit` beside the table moves the panels and the table together, from none to the 64 of the sample.

## Fixed

- **A change of `draws` left the band of the number before it drawn** ([#6](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim-web/pull/6)). Nothing watched the attribute, so a page which turned the draws off kept its bands and a page which turned them up kept the old band until the next run of the panel for another reason. A panel schedules a run on a change of it now, and a frozen panel owes it as it owes a movement of a knob.
