# Release notes for sbmlsim-web 0.6.0

The drawing of a model is read at the size its package drew it: a click enlarges it to the window, and the column it sits in grows with the page instead of staying at 560 px. A page which draws a figure is a dashboard: its panels are plots and nothing else, the reader clears what the drawing picked, and a run which is frozen stays in every plot as a dashed curve while the knobs move on.

## Features

- **A click enlarges the drawing ([#12](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim-web/pull/12)).** A model drawing is dense and wide, and in the column of a page its labels are small: the anatomical figure of a whole-body model is about 1900 px wide, which is a third of that in the column, where a reader can neither read an organ nor aim at one. `<sbmlsim-figure>` now shows the drawing in a modal dialog at the size of the window. The button in the corner of the figure opens it, and so does a click on any place of the drawing which opens no panel, which is everything that is not a control. A click on a control of the enlarged drawing reports its path and closes the dialog, so a reader aims at an organ in the large drawing and lands back on the panels it opened; a click beside the drawing, the close button and Escape close it without reporting anything.

  The drawing is not copied into the dialog, it moves there and back, so the ids inside the svg stay unique, the controls and the marks are the ones the page decorated and one listener serves both places. The figure keeps the height the drawing had while it is away, so the page behind the dialog does not jump. The new parts are `zoom`, `overlay`, `stage` and `close`.

- **The figure column grows with the page ([#12](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim-web/pull/12)).** A page with a figure gave it a column of 560 px and every pixel beyond that to the panels, so a wider window made the drawing no bigger. The column is now half of the shell, from 560 px up to 1100 px, and the grid beside it keeps the room of a card of 480 px at every width the column is given; below 1088 px of shell the figure is above the grid, centred, as before.

- **A run which is frozen stays in the plots ([#13](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim-web/pull/13)).** A reader who wants to see what a knob does has had to remember the curve it drew before, which is what a comparison is not. The card of a page now has a Freeze, which keeps the curves of every panel of the page as they are: they stay in their plots as dashed curves in the colours they were drawn in, with the axis of every figure holding them, while the knobs move on and the live curves are drawn solid over them. Up to four runs are kept, each with a dash of its own, and the card shows one chip per run with that dash, the state of the shared knobs it was frozen at and a cross which drops it; the chips go with a switch of the page or of the variant, since the curves of a run belong to the panels and the model it was frozen in. A plot of one curve, which draws no legend of its own, keeps a legend while a run of it is frozen, so the live curve and the frozen one are named. `<sbmlsim-panel>` carries the runs: `keep(id, rank, label)`, `drop(id)` and `clearKept()`, and `PlotSpec.frozen` is what the renderer draws. The comparison controls, the Clear selection of a page with a figure, the Freeze and the chips, are a line of their own at every width, as the line of the run is, so a control which comes and goes adds no line to the card. The new parts are `settings-freeze`, `settings-frozen` and `settings-compare`.

- **The panels of a page with a figure are plots and nothing else ([#13](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim-web/pull/13)).** On such a page the card above the grid already carries the logarithmic switch, the draws, the Freeze, the Reset and the line of the last run, so every card below repeated them in a panel of 300 px: the new property `compact` of `<sbmlsim-panel>` draws a card without its options, without the line of its run and with its own controls only where it has knobs of its own, and the shell sets it on every panel of a page which draws a figure. A page without a figure is the panel as it was.

- **The panels the drawing picked are cleared again ([#13](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim-web/pull/13)).** A click on the drawing replaces the panels of the page, and the way back was to find the part the page started on. The card of a page with a figure now has a Clear selection, which shows the panels the page declares and forgets what was picked, dimmed while nothing is picked. The new part is `settings-clear`.

- **The information of an app takes what its page adds ([#13](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim-web/pull/13)).** The page which hosts `<sbmlsim-app>` has its own notices to show, the licenses of the engine which simulates in the browser among them, and a footer of its own was the wrong place for them: the information view now ends in the slot `licenses`, whose content the page writes and styles, next to the licenses of the model. The new part is `information-host`.
