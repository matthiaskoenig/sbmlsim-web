# Release notes for sbmlsim-web 0.4.1

A panel whose model carries no uncertainty draws its curves again, which 0.4.0 broke for every panel of a model without a fit.

## Fixed

- **A panel of a model without an uncertainty drew nothing** ([#8](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim-web/pull/8)). The service of sbmlsim answers `uncertainty: null` for a panel whose model carries none, and a service before sbmlsim 0.30 answers nothing at all; the draws of a request read only the second spelling, so the first threw where it should have asked for no draw and the panel showed no curve. Every panel of a model without a fit is affected, the app pages of hctz and apixaban of pkdb_models among them. The end to end tests ran against sbmlsim 0.27.1, whose service has no such field, which is why 0.4.0 shipped it: they run against 0.31.0 now, and against the released bundle 61 of their 77 cases fail.

## Changes

- **The mirror of the contract follows the service** ([#8](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim-web/pull/8)). The uncertainty of a declaration and of a run is `PanelUncertainty | null` and `Uncertainty | null`, the dosing protocols of a panel are a type of their own (`PanelDosing`, which the service has sent since sbmlsim 0.28 and the mirror never carried), and `src/fixtures/contract.json` is of the service of sbmlsim 0.31.0.
- **The assets of the glimepiride example are of the releases of the chain** ([#8](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim-web/pull/8)). They are rebuilt from sbmlsim 0.31.0, sbmlsim-core 0.12.0 and the glimepiride model of pkdb_models which declares the uncertainty of its fit.
