# Release notes for sbmlsim-web 0.1.0

The first release of sbmlsim-web, the renderer of the interactive panels of an SBML model. A model declares its panels in its own SBML, an endpoint of sbmlsim (`python -m sbmlsim.serve`, sbmlsim >= 0.24.0) serves the declarations and runs their simulations, and two custom elements draw them in any HTML page: `<sbmlsim-panel>`, one panel with its knobs, its curves and the study data under them, and `<sbmlsim-app>`, the pages of the panels of a model with their shared knobs, their scenarios and the variants of the model. A page loads one script and writes one tag, in a documentation site, a slide deck or a course.

## Features

- **Two custom elements and one script.** `<sbmlsim-panel>` and `<sbmlsim-app>` register themselves on import, with Vue inlined in every bundle: an ES module, a UMD bundle for a `require` and an IIFE bundle for a plain `<script src>`, which is also what a CDN serves (`https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/sbmlsim-web@0.1.0/dist/sbmlsim-web.iife.js`). The types give a TypeScript host the methods of the element of each tag.
- **Three versioned contracts and nothing else.** The client speaks `panel/1`, `paneldata/1` and `app/1` of the service, checks the version and the shape of every payload, retries a lost request once and a request the service sheds (a 503 with a `Retry-After`) after the time it names, and resolves every failure into one line a reader can act on, which names no url and no host. A payload of another version is refused.
- **A grid per figure, not a request per movement.** A run grids over one knob, and every movement of that knob is read out of the grid; releasing another knob runs one simulation which grids over that one instead.
- **The figure of a report.** The renderer draws a figure as one SVG string - axes with units, curves, study data with error bars, a legend or a colour bar - laid out with the widths of the font the page resolves, with tick labels which carry the digits that tell their ticks apart.
- **The colour scheme of the page.** Every colour is a pair resolved against the colour scheme the page declares, so a panel is light on a light page and dark on a dark one whatever the system of the reader is, and paints its own surface; every text colour meets 4.5:1 and every line 3:1 against its surface in both schemes. `--sbmlsim-*` tokens restyle a panel from any ancestor.
- **Accessible values.** The figure is a slider over its time axis whose value is the readout in words, so a reader of the keyboard or of a screen reader reads every value of every curve; the controls are named after their panel.
- **Made for decks.** `activate()` and `freeze()` let a deck start the panel of the shown slide and stop the others; a panel the deck passed over while it loaded finishes when its slide comes back, and the keys of a knob and of the figure do not turn the slide.

## Embedding

The README has a quick start for a page with a CDN script, the `--allow-origin` of the service, a Zensical site, a reveal.js deck and a Moodle course.

## Requirements

- An endpoint of sbmlsim >= 0.24.0.
- Chrome or Edge 123, Firefox 120 or Safari 17.5, or newer.
