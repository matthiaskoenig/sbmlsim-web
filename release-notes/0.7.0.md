# Release notes for sbmlsim-web 0.7.0

A panel states what its curves mean in numbers: the peak of the exposure, when it is reached, how much of it there is over the window, and how long it takes to halve.

## Features

- **A panel states the pharmacokinetic parameters of its curves ([#15](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim-web/pull/15)).** A reader of a curve of a concentration asks how high it went, when, how much of it there was and how long it takes to halve, and read those off the plot by eye until now. A panel whose declaration carries `pk` draws a table under its figure, one row per curve: `C_max` and `t_max` off the points, the area under the curve over the window of the plot by the linear trapezoid, and the half-life of the terminal phase. It is a non-compartmental analysis of what is drawn, no fit and no model, and the row carries the colour of the curve it describes, so the table and the legend are read together.

  The parameters are read off the curve of the position the knobs are at, the one the reader sees, so the table moves with the figure and never states a number of another state. The terminal phase is the log-linear fit of the tail with the best adjusted coefficient of determination over every window of at least three positive points after the peak, which is the rule of a non-compartmental analysis; a curve whose tail does not fall - a cumulative amount in the urine, a curve still rising at the end of the window - carries no such phase, and its half-life is none rather than a number read off a fit which does not describe it. A curve which no analysis describes leaves no row, and a panel whose curves leave none draws no table.

  The table sits inside the figure and under the plot, since a panel is three rows of the subgrid of a card of the app (its head, its body and its foot): everything a panel draws under its plot belongs in the body, and a fourth row would be drawn over the foot of the card. The new part is `pk`.

  The flag of the service is `PanelTexts.pk`, which sbmlsim 0.35 writes and no service before it carries, so a panel of an older service is a panel as it was.

- **The analysis is a module of its own.** `src/pk.ts` is pure, as `interpolate.ts` is: `pkOf(x, y)` gives the parameters of a curve, `summary(values, level)` the median and the two percentiles of a level over the draws of a parameter, for the interval a table states once the curves of the draws travel with an answer of the position a reader is at. The band of a grid is a percentile of every point and not of a parameter, so the table of a gridded panel states the parameters of the curve it draws.

## Tests

Ten tests of the analysis against a Bateman curve whose peak, its time, the area and the half-life are known in closed form, and four of the table in the panel: that it is absent without the flag, that it names every curve which is drawn, that the swatch of a row is the colour of its curve, and that a curve which does not fall states no half-life. 578 tests pass.
